# 摘要与关键词

## 中文摘要

生成式语音增强需要在提高声音质量的同时保持输入内容，但感知代理、信号重建与真实内容恢复并不等价。本文以本地指定的StoRM/SGMSE判别式前端与扩散后端系统为对象，研究冻结基座上的局部路由权衡、内容评价的证据边界，以及受控响应训练的独立增量。首先，描述已实现Router的动作、未来重评分监督与成本，并整理同一checkpoint上的模块开关评价。结果呈现条件性多目标权衡：无混响下部分重建和质量代理改善，部分感知代理下降；混响下亦未形成综合优势。其次，结合自然句探索、条件干预、结构读出与单人参照辅助观察，区分结构可听、识别器输出和具体音素证据；这些材料不足以证明真实音素恢复或幻觉降低。最后，CPEL分别检验强共同点clean回归基线上的成对约束增量（M/P），以及普通反事实DSM之外的增量（N/D），各臂均训练500更新。两轮留出响应代理改善均未达预定10%筛查门；真实public部署下D相对N的SI-SDR仅增加约0.00459dB，未建立实质综合增强或内容收益。两种评价接口分开报告，当前固定配方关闭，不外推为所有反事实训练无效。固定64条Clean的tiny.en词读出仅49条匹配，未满足该诊断的适用标准，未用于含噪或增强输出评价。受限于探索材料与评价协议，现有结果明确了指定系统的条件性行为与代理评价限制，尚未确认独立新机制收益，内容、身份、质量与效率的综合目标仍未实现。未来研究需以可靠的独立输出内容读数或不同且可证伪的机制依据支撑，有限负筛查不否定所有研究方向。

**关键词**：语音增强；扩散模型；内容保真；可靠性路由；反事实响应

## English Abstract

Generative speech enhancement must improve sound quality while preserving input content, yet perceptual proxies, signal reconstruction, and genuine content recovery are distinct objectives. This study examines a specified local StoRM/SGMSE system with a discriminative frontend and a diffusion backend, focusing on routing tradeoffs, the evidential limits of content evaluation, and the incremental value of controlled response training. First, it describes the implemented Router, its future rescoring supervision, and computational costs, and consolidates module activation comparisons within the same checkpoint. Results show conditional tradeoffs: some reconstruction and quality proxies improve without reverberation while other perceptual proxies decline; reverberant evaluation also provides no comprehensive advantage. Second, exploratory natural sentences, condition interventions, structural readouts, and reference-assisted observations from one listener distinguish audible structure, recognizer outputs, and phoneme-specific evidence. These materials do not establish genuine phoneme recovery or reduced hallucination. Finally, CPEL tests paired constraints both above a strong common-state pointwise clean-regression baseline (M/P) and beyond ordinary counterfactual DSM (N/D), with 500 updates per arm. Neither comparison reaches the predefined 10% held-out response-proxy improvement threshold. At the actual public deployment entry, D improves SI-SDR over N by only approximately 0.00459 dB, without establishing substantial combined enhancement or content benefits. The two evaluation interfaces are reported separately; the fixed recipe is closed without rejecting all counterfactual training. A fixed tiny.en word readout matches only 49 of 64 clean recordings, fails its applicability criterion, and is not applied to noisy or enhanced outputs. Within these exploratory materials and evaluation protocols, results characterize conditional system behavior and evaluation limitations without confirming independent benefits from a new mechanism. The combined content, identity, quality, and efficiency objectives remain unmet. Future work requires reliable independent output-content readouts or a different falsifiable mechanistic basis; bounded negative screening does not invalidate all research directions.

**Keywords**: speech enhancement; diffusion models; content fidelity; reliability routing; counterfactual response
