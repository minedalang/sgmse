Blind A/B listening pilot

Exploratory pilot: 8 utterances from a repeatedly used development set, rated by one listener. This is for qualitative diagnosis only, not an independent test or a population-level claim. Do not open the separate scoring key before finishing ratings. Keep playback volume fixed; do not loudness-normalize either side.

For each trial, compare A and B and select the one that sounds more natural and less distorted overall, or choose tie/no preference. Consider residual noise, metallic/robotic sound, interruptions, timbre, and intelligibility. Do not try to guess which system produced a version. Record one response per trial.
